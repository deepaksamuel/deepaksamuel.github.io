Deepak Samuel

Experimental high-energy physicist · Associate Professor, Central University of Karnataka

Deepak Samuel deepaksamuel@gmail.com · GitHub · LinkedIn · Google Scholar

I am an experimental physicist specialising in neutrino physics, cosmic muons and proton therapy, with more than a decade of work on particle detectors, nuclear instrumentation, Monte Carlo simulation, high-performance computing and data analysis. I currently explore the intersections of machine learning with particle physics with my current work focussing of transformer models for particle track finding.

Research & collaborations

Collaborator since 2008 India-based Neutrino Observatory

A mega-science project with a proposed budget of ₹2000 Cr, now shelved, and the focus of my research until recently: detector development, machine-learning applications, and searches for new event topologies.

Institutional Board Member since 2020 Electron-Ion Collider (BNL, USA)

An upcoming experiment on the spatial and spin structure of gluons in the proton based at BNL, USA, and part of India’s Mega Science Vision-2035 in nuclear physics. I contributed to the background estimates for the ATHENA detector and now working on the simulation studies of the dRICH subsystem in ePIC specifically on the a vision transformer for particle-identification for the dRICH.

Institutional PI since 2025 Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (Fermilab, USA)

An upcoming neutrino-oscillation experiment based at FermiLab, DUNE and part of India's Mega Science Vision-2035 in high-energy physics. The Indian team in which I participate, will contribute to the building of Straw Tube Trackers for the near detector,

Institutional PI since 2021 J-PARC muon g−2/EDM (J-PARC, Japan)

Positron track-finding strategies under pile-up conditions, including GPU-based track finding. Options for long-term participation are being explored.

Experience

Jan 2020 – present Associate Professor, Central University of Karnataka

Teaching Quantum Mechanics, Electronics and Nuclear Physics to undergraduate and postgraduate students in addition to my research on EIC detector simulation and track reconstruction for the J-PARC muon g−2 experiment.

Jul 2019 – Dec 2019 Associate Professor, Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics, Kolkata

A short tenure working on neutrino physics and dark matter.

Mar 2014 – Jun 2019 Assistant Professor, Central University of Karnataka

Taught Quantum Mechanics, Electronics and Nuclear Physics to M.Sc. and B.Tech. students, alongside research supervision.

2012 – 2014 Postdoctoral researcher, UCL Belgium & Harvard Medical School

Developed an in-vivo range-verification system for proton therapy of prostate cancer: the detectors, the data-acquisition system, and the software used in treatment rooms.

2008 – 2012 Postdoctoral researcher, TIFR Mumbai (INO)

Built the data-acquisition system for the prototype muon telescope and helped develop Resistive Plate Chambers. Tools I wrote for cosmic-muon analysis and simulation are still used by the collaboration. I also led much of the outreach in Tamil Nadu.
